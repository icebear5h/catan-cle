## Failure patterns

- `color_road_locations` remained effectively ungrounded. Eight of ten answers were the same incorrect `<E03_17>` token; one was `NONE`, and one fabricated a short edge sequence.
- `node_occupancy` scored zero. The model answered `EMPTY` seven times and the unsupported label `<TOWN>` three times.
- `port_trade_type` answered `GENERIC 3:1` on all ten questions, obtaining five exact matches from the target distribution rather than reliable port reading.
- `port_occupancy` answered `NONE` eight times, obtaining three exact matches.
- `tile_has_robber` answered `NO` nine times and `YES` once. All ten selected questions were positive cases, so only the lone `YES` was correct.
- `robber_resource_number` never bound all three requested components correctly. It frequently assigned a normal resource/number to the robber tile when the target was desert.
- Building and road counts were usually returned as bare integers. The scorer accepts a response when every requested semantic component is present, so these are not penalized merely for omitting decorative tokens.

Qwen3.8-27B is the strongest Qwen result among these comparable raw visual runs and follows the short-answer instruction far better than Qwen3-VL-32B: 578 completion tokens with no runaway response versus 49,528 tokens dominated by five runaway edge enumerations. It still does not provide dependable node, road-location, robber, or port grounding and remains below Fable's accuracy ceiling.
